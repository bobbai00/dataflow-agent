### Opus48 archeology-hard-7 — none→flow GAIN — VARIANCE
Question: how many modern cities with population over 100k lie within 0.1 degrees of a Roman-era city? Gold: 274. Off arm (none) answered: 295. On arm (flow) answered: 274.
Gold plan: roman_cities.csv (`Longitude (X)`, `Latitude (Y)`); worldcities.csv filtered to `population > 100000`; `cKDTree.query_ball_point(r=0.1)`, a Euclidean radius in (lng, lat) degrees; count the world cities with at least one Roman city inside the radius.
Hinge: none arm, call 3 (`count_matches`): it counted a city as matched when `(dlat <= tol) & (dlng <= tol)`. That is a 0.2°×0.2° square (Chebyshev distance), not the Euclidean radius, and it admits 21 extra cities.
Evidence at hinge:
- none, call 3: `"""Count modern cities within tol degrees (both lat and lng) of any Roman city."""` / `if np.any((dlat <= tol) & (dlng <= tol)):` → `| 295 |`. Only the call-1 scan samples were in context; this arm has no flow lines.
- flow, call 2, where the metric was chosen: "Filter big cities, cross join with Roman cities, keep those within 0.1 degrees Euclidean, count distinct modern cities." / `return (dlat*dlat + dlng*dlng) ** 0.5 <= degrees`. The only flow lines in context were `- flow: Scan parsed selected sources; files selected=1/1, omitted=0; output=1388 rows.` (and 44691 rows for world).
Why: The metric was fixed before any non-trivial flow line existed. The later filter/join flow lines came after the decision, and the model then answered directly. The stats arm (no flow) used Euclidean inside a custom Python function (`if np.any(dlat*dlat + dlng*dlng <= thr2):`, docstring "Euclidean in lat/lng") and passed. The both arm also passed. The flow arm took a built-in route (cross join + predicate filter + distinct), but the stats arm passed on the custom-Python route, so the route did not decide the outcome. Only the none arm drew the box.

### Opus48 environment-hard-7 — none→flow GAIN — VARIANCE
Question: what is the difference in 2023 marine-beach bacterial exceedance rates between communities with more than 50% and less than 25% EJ population? Gold: 10.87. Off (none): 10.24. On (flow): 10.87.
Gold plan: water-body-testing-2023 filtered to `Beach Type Description == 'Marine'`; EJ table split on `Percent of population in EJ BGs` > 50 and < 25; INNER merge on Community = Municipality, so unmatched communities drop out; rate = share of `Violation == 'yes'` × 100; take the difference.
Hinge: none arm, call 7 (`classify`). Its own coverage check had found 19 marine communities missing from the EJ file. It then left-joined and set `pct = pct.fillna(0.0)`, which put those communities' 1,373 samples into the <25% group. The low group became 4,668 samples with 228 exceedances (4.88%) instead of 3,295 with 140 (4.25%).
Evidence at hinge:
- none, call 7 reasoning: "The 19 unmatched communities are simply not in the EJ file (no EJ designation). For the analysis, these should be treated as communities with 0% EJ (less than 25%)." Code: `set msamp: join(marine, ej, on=[('Community','Municipality')], how='left')` + `pct = pct.fillna(0.0)` → `| low | 4668 | 228 | 0.0488431876606684 |`. Context: its own `nomatch` retrieval (19 rows: Wellfleet, Swansea, Somerset, …); no flow lines.
- flow, call 3: `set joined: join(marine, ejp, on=[('Community','Municipality')], how='inner')`, written before any join flow line existed. Afterwards it saw `- flow: left matched rows=7118/8491, unmatched rows=1373; matched keys=42/61, unmatched keys=19; …` and `- flow: left eligible unmatched-key examples (not exhaustive): ["Wellfleet"] × 116; ["Swansea"] × 65; ["Somerset"] × 15.` Its next reasoning was only "Compute exceedance rates per group and the difference between high (>50%) and low (<25%)."
Why: The flow arm chose the inner join before any join flow content appeared. When the flow lines later reported the same 19 unmatched communities the none arm had found on its own, the model did not mention them. So flow content neither prompted nor prevented the 0% imputation. The stats arm (no flow) ran the same anti-join check, saw the same communities, and weighed the "0% EJ" reading. It kept the inner join anyway: "The unmatched communities are not in the comparison regardless since they have no EJ percent. The result stands." This is an interpretation coin flip at a real ambiguity.

Flow content did not decide any of these 12 divergences: 11 are VARIANCE and 1 is EVAL-ARTIFACT, with no KNOB-MISLED, KNOB-HELPED, KNOB-ROUTE or KNOB-BUDGET. In 10 flips the deciding code or answer was written when the only flow lines in context were scan file/row counts, group or filter counts unrelated to the decision, or "row/column mapping unknown" boilerplate; in wildfire-hard-7 there were none at all. Three times a flow line carried exactly the fact that mattered, and Opus did not act on it: "unmatched keys=19" in environment-hard-7 (flow arm), Nashville listed as an unmatched key in legal-hard-1 (both arm), and `"Case_excluded" … "No":12, "Yes":2` in biomedical-easy-2 (flow-only arm). The mechanism is the same in both pairs. Each flip is a single arm deviating from the other three dataflow arms; none shows the flow arms splitting against the non-flow arms. In none→flow, the none arm is the lone dataflow failure on all 5 gains. In stats→both, the both arm is the lone failure on 3 losses and the lone pass on the gain, and the stats arm is the lone pass on the other 3 losses (legal-hard-18, legal-hard-29, wildfire-hard-18), where the none and/or flow arms fail the same way. So "+5/−0 without statistics, +1/−6 with statistics" records which single arm drew badly or luckily, not a flow effect that changes sign when statistics are present. The only pair difference visible in the traces is redundancy: with statistics on, the flow lines near a hinge mostly repeated what sample_stats already showed (`nonnull=13/14`, `groups=8`). Both comparisons also cross campaign runs (none and both ran in 2arms-a, stats and flow in 2arms-b); the traces show ordinary reasoning choices, so they give no run-level cause beyond that coincidence.
