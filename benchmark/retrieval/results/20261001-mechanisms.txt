KramaBench retrieval and flow evidence: failure audit — 2026-10-01

Conclusion
----------
The mixed accuracy results involve both model variability and systematic issues.
The evidence reached the model, and the independently checked statistics were
correct. However, the profile budget discarded useful categorical frequencies,
the flow selector hid some measured conversion losses, and Luna sometimes
stopped before making any tool call. Other failures persisted despite correct,
visible evidence: wrong populations, grouping keys, formulas, and final-answer
selection. These mechanisms should not be collapsed into a claim that flow
evidence either helps or hurts accuracy.

The original 1,248-attempt campaign is unchanged. The two subsequent pilots are
diagnostics on five selected tasks, not new full-benchmark accuracy estimates.
No failed attempt was replaced. No task-specific answer rule was added.

1. Did the model receive the statistics?
---------------------------------------
Yes, when the renderer selected them. Across the original campaign there were
6,770 successful retrieves. Of these, 6,765 had a subsequent recorded model
request, and all 6,765 observation bodies appeared verbatim in that request's
tool results. Five final observations had no subsequent request. No missing
delivery was found. This establishes availability, not correct use.

The enabled prompts already described data and flow evidence, partial coverage,
unknown statistics, non-random retained samples, narrow projections, native
operators, eligibility, and rounding. The problem was not simply absent guidance.

Tasks receiving at least one data-statistics block, out of 104 per arm:

                     sample_stats, no flow     sample_stats, with flow
Luna                           81                         90
Terra                          85                         83

Thus 339/416 statistics-arm attempts received data facts. Others inspected only
raw previews or complete small tables, or stopped early. Raw lines have no parsed
column statistics; complete small tables intentionally omit redundant data facts.
Enabling sample_stats is not equivalent to receiving statistics on every call.

The original 4,922 context-prefix checks had zero failures. Original final
snapshots also had zero count/version invariant violations over 11,547 profiles
and 104,356 columns. There is no evidence here of a prompt-prefix or stale-profile
bug explaining the score differences. These checks do not prove every cache
path correct or identify undocumented provider-side behavior.

2. Are the statistics reasonable?
---------------------------------
Independent pandas recomputation checked counts, numeric ranges, and every
published value frequency for three source scans: 480 comparisons, zero
mismatches. Scope:

Source/task                Source rows  Profile rows  Profile columns  Checks
worldcities / archeology-easy-11 44,691       16,384           11           61
report categories / legal-easy-26   524          524            4           35
clinical metadata / biomedical-easy-2
                                  153          153          128          384

The city profile is explicitly a prefix, not the full 44,691-row distribution.
The workbook has 179 columns; the profiler records up to 128. These limits are
reported. Literal strings such as "NA" are not nulls under the parser's policy;
nullCount=0 therefore does not imply every value is analytically usable.
The source checks are deliberately limited to known simple parse settings.

A systematic problem was loss of useful frequencies under the payload cap.
In final snapshots, 677 profiles across 350 attempts dropped all frequencies
after exceeding the profile budget. This removed 38,901 column summaries,
including 17,018 columns with only 1–8 distinct values. Many such columns are
useful category/eligibility flags, although low cardinality alone does not
establish relevance.

Reprofiling the same 153-row clinical workbook illustrates the repair:

                        Before          After
Serialized profile      45,377 bytes    64,960 bytes (cap: 65,536)
Columns with frequencies     0              60
Case_excluded values    absent          No:144, Yes:9, other:0

The profiler now removes the largest value dictionaries first and preserves
compact categories where possible. The renderer reserves space for frequencies
and ranges instead of allowing count lines to consume the entire display budget.
Limits still apply; neither all columns nor all frequencies are guaranteed.

3. Systematic flow and protocol issues
-------------------------------------
Conversion loss was measured but often hidden by selection. In Luna's
legal-easy-26 failure, casting comma-formatted report counts with errors="null"
lost 317/520 values. The final result retained only four states. The flow selector
previously omitted Cast ancestors and stopped after three ordinary ancestors,
so intervening projections/filters obscured this measurement.

There were nonzero cast-loss counters in 106 attempts' final snapshots. Some
losses are intentional; this count is not a count of wrong answers. Manual
audits confirmed harmful losses in legal-easy-26, legal-hard-14 (150/660), and
legal-hard-24 (14/26 in the selected Florida table).

The selector now considers ancestors within its existing depth/node bounds and
prioritizes measured conversion losses. Replaying the new renderer on those
three frozen failed results exposes all three warnings without re-executing the
graphs. It still respects version bindings and opaque Process boundaries.
Model-authored operator descriptions are no longer labeled as engine guarantees.

Luna also had 23 original attempts without Answer: 18 stopped on the first
plain-text response, three stopped later, and two exhausted the step limit.
Terra had none. The 18 first-response failures received no tool evidence, so
their outcomes cannot be attributed to the content of delivered statistics.
The driver requested toolChoice="required"; retained traces cannot identify
which upstream model/proxy component produced the contrary response.

Six of the 15 sample-only -> sample_stats Luna pass gains coincided with a
no-answer failure in the sample-only run. Eight of the 15 sample-only ->
sample+flow gains did too. This materially confounds an interpretation that
those gains all came from better data analysis. In contrast, all 19 pass flips
between Luna stats without/with flow (six gains, 13 losses) had actual answers.

A normal plain-text stop now gets one recorded correction asking for a Dataflow
action. It uses the remaining original model-step budget and retains all usage.
It does not restart the task, clear state, extend the step limit, or retry an
answer judged wrong. Refusals, aborts and exhausted step budgets do not trigger
this correction. Feedback remains chronological so it cannot replace the user
task or rewrite the cacheable prefix. The latter was a problem discovered while
testing the new correction, not a cause of the original campaign's regressions.

These are not corrupt summary statistics. Some omissions hid useful warnings;
others involved correct evidence being ignored or a wrong analytical definition.
The numerical-loss mechanism also appears in Terra legal-hard-14; eligibility
and case handling recur in Terra biomedical-hard-5 and environment-hard-18.

Checks of gains give the same caution. On environment-easy-1, both Luna runs
had 738 violations / 15,388 samples. No-flow miscomputed/rounded the ratio to
4.795; flow returned 4.796. On legal-hard-18, the with-flow run repaired parsing
and its denominator to return 91,000; the no-flow run algebraically cancelled
its own denominator and returned 473,000. Neither contrast alone establishes
that flow caused the successful reasoning.

5. Repairs and diagnostic results
---------------------------------
41a3a4516: preserve compact frequencies; prioritize measured upstream cast loss;
reserve display space for useful values/ranges; separate authored descriptions
from guarantees; clarify eligibility, missing markers and numeric normalization.

484c6f3d9: one bounded missing-action correction with preserved usage and prefix.

b6a3c7234 (after the pilots): accept explicitly supplied Scan defaults such as
file_column=None, which the signature advertised but validation rejected;
clarify that Answer must use the retrieved result matching the final
interpretation. These final small changes are unit-tested but their accuracy
effect has not been measured by another live pilot.

6. What can be claimed, and the next experiment
----------------------------------------------
We can claim that evidence is delivered, tested statistics match their source
within documented coverage, and the fixes retain useful information under a
fixed display budget. We have a live trace where an exposed cast warning is
followed by the corresponding repair. We cannot claim that these diagnostics
establish an accuracy gain across KramaBench or that stats+flow dominates other
views. The original full-run results remain the paper's current full-run data.

A clean follow-up should freeze the corrected engine/protocol for every arm,
cross prompt guidance with evidence on/off, and use several matched independent
repetitions. Keep all attempts and cost, including protocol corrections. Report
official scores, pass rates, paired task differences, protocol failures, and
observed warning-to-repair behavior separately. Bootstrap over tasks and estimate
run-to-run variance rather than treating one repeat as conclusive. Keep a task
set untouched by this diagnostic tuning before making a general accuracy claim.
