  It saw Case_excluded frequencies (No:144, Yes:9), then projected only age

  exclusion flag. Haiku repeatedly checked arithmetic without correcting

  The cast's 52 missing ages, aggregate values and evidence were correct.

  evidence; lack of evidence delivery does not explain this failure.

Failure: legal-hard-14
  Haiku's first Process assumed raw-preview columns file/line, while the full
  scan returned parsed table columns. Execute and Retrieve reported the same
  underlying KeyError. Haiku recovered by retrieving the independent scan
  and rebuilding the source with a file column.
  Its replacement Process used pd.to_numeric(errors='coerce') without
  removing thousands separators. Independently reading all 52 source files
  finds 452 valid numeric report rows, including 150 comma-formatted values.
  The Process retained exactly 302 rows, losing those 150 records.
  Filename-derived NewHampshire and RhodeIsland also failed to match the
  spaced state names. Native join evidence showed only four of six right-side
  states matched; Haiku did not repair this warning. Its final top five
  therefore omitted the official top five metropolitan areas.
  The custom Process bundled filtering and coercion despite native-operator
  guidance. Flow metadata cannot identify all operations inside custom code.
