KramaBench retrieval × flow: full campaign launch, 2026-10-01

Protocol
--------
All 104 original top-level tasks, six domains, original KramaBench evaluator.
Models: gpt-5.6-luna and gpt-5.6-terra.
Retrieval: full, sample, sample_stats, each crossed with flowEvidence false/true.
12 model/configuration cells × 104 tasks × one repetition = 1,248 attempts.
The sample_stats + flow cell is included for both models.
No outcome-selected retries. Failures and timeouts remain recorded.

Sample records: at most 2,000 characters and five rows per retrieved operator.
Full records: at most 100,000 characters per retrieved operator.
Data statistics: a separate 2,000-character budget.
Flow evidence: a separate 2,000-character budget.
Evidence does not displace the sample rows. Retrieval is fixed per cell;
this campaign does not contain an adaptive-choice arm.
Native mode, DELTA context, 25 agent steps, profile collection enabled in
all cells, thought replay and code in snapshots disabled. These controls
match the earlier native campaign and retrieval pilots.

Pass threshold: original official score >= 1.0. Report mean official scores
as well as full-pass counts. Cost: stats.json cost_usd, including cached-input
discounts; evaluator calls and engine resources are not included in model cost.
No full-benchmark result is available at launch.

Launch anomaly and repair
-------------------------
A first launch at 751b631fe was stopped after 21 attempts when manual review
of Luna / sample_flow1 / legal-easy-12 found incorrect scan-repair evidence.
The hint reported "# of Reports" while the actual column was " # of Reports ".
The agent followed that hint and received another MISSING_COLUMN error.
Repair 21fb91a77 preserves exact column names in scan suggestions while keeping
normalized names for heuristic detection. Regression validation: 34 Python
scan/check tests and six TypeScript evidence/renderer tests passed.

All 21 attempts are preserved as diagnostics and excluded from this campaign:
/tmp/texera-retrieval-full-20261001-results
.claude/artifacts/retrieval-full-diagnostic-20261001
Their model cost was $0.392015. Their six computing units were terminated after
active attempts finished. The corrected cohort starts every cell afresh; it
does not select better diagnostic answers or mix agent revisions.
